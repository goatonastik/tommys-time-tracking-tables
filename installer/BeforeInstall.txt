Tommy's Time Tracking Tables - Preview

Before installing or updating, save your timers and close any running copy of the app.

Setup installs for your Windows account and includes the .NET runtime. A Start menu shortcut is included; a desktop shortcut is optional.

Your timers, settings and backups are stored separately in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TimersApp

Uninstalling the app preserves that saved data.

This preview installer is not code-signed. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
